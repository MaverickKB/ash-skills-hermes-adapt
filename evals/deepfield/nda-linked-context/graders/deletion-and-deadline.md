PASS only if the reply applies D003 Section S2's 24-hour notice rule over D001's 48-hour rule and identifies 11 June 2025 at 14:00 in the detection's unstated local time zone as the latest corresponding time, without inventing a UTC offset. It must say destruction is not necessarily immediate or absolute: D001 Section 5 requires prompt destruction and certification within ten business days, permits one legally required archival copy, and D003 Sections S3 and S4 permit qualifying backups for at most 35 days and active written legal holds. It must include disaster-recovery re-deletion before ordinary access resumes, continuing confidentiality, and deletion within five business days after a hold is released.
