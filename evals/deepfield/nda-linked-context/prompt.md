--- D001: MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT, PDF p. 1 ---

Section 1. Parties and Purpose. Northstar Labs and Copper Fjord Systems may exchange information solely to evaluate a distributed-storage partnership (the "Purpose"). A party receiving Confidential Information is the "Receiving Party," and the party disclosing it is the "Disclosing Party."

Section 2. Confidential Information. "Confidential Information" means nonpublic information marked confidential or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given its nature and the circumstances of disclosure. It excludes information shown by written records to be public without breach, already lawfully known without a duty, independently developed without use of the information, or lawfully received from a third party without a duty.

Section 3. Use and Disclosure. The Receiving Party may use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose. It may disclose Confidential Information only to employees and professional advisers with a need to know for the Purpose who are bound by written confidentiality obligations at least as protective as this Agreement. The Receiving Party remains liable for their breaches. It must not disclose Confidential Information to contractors without the Disclosing Party's prior written consent.

Section 4. Compelled Disclosure. A legally compelled disclosure is permitted only to the extent required. Where lawful, the Receiving Party must give written notice at least five business days before disclosure and reasonably cooperate with protective-order efforts. If the legally allowed period is shorter, it must give prompt notice before disclosure.

Section 5. Return and Destruction. After a written request, the Receiving Party must promptly return or destroy Confidential Information and certify destruction within ten business days. It may retain one archival copy required by law and automatic backup copies under Schedule S-1. Retained information remains confidential and must not be accessed in the ordinary course.

Section 6. Term and Survival. This Agreement starts on 1 February 2025 and expires eighteen months later unless earlier terminated on thirty days' written notice. Either party may terminate as provided in the preceding sentence. Confidentiality obligations survive for three years after expiration or termination. For trade secrets, those obligations continue for as long as the information remains a trade secret under applicable law.

Section 7. Liability Cap. Except for fraud or wilful misconduct, each party's aggregate liability arising out of this Agreement is capped at USD 100,000.

Section 8. Security. The Receiving Party must comply with Schedule S-1 and Control Catalogue CC-4, both incorporated by reference. It must notify the Disclosing Party of a security incident within forty-eight hours after detection.

Section 9. Precedence and Amendments. A signed amendment controls over this Agreement. A schedule controls over this Agreement only where the schedule expressly states the override. All other provisions remain unchanged unless amended in a signed writing.
